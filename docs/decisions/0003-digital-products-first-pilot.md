# Decision 0003 - Digital Products First Pilot

Date: 2026-07-06
Status: completed historical pilot direction; superseded as a current portfolio
priority

## Status Update - 2026-07-29

The review trigger was reached. Pantheon proved a digital-product engineering
path and later ran one bounded buyer-intent preparation path to its terminal
quality stop. Those records remain useful evidence, but they did not produce a
buyer, revenue, or positive net cash contribution.

Future opportunities and channels are selected through current commercial
evidence rather than a standing digital-product-first rule. Decision 0009
selects a separate low-touch digital-kit hypothesis for preparation; that
specific choice does not restore digital products as Pantheon's generic
portfolio priority or reopen the stopped build.

## Original Decision

Make digital products the first commercial pilot direction before returning to
POD/Gelato supplier-push publishing.

## Context

The operator wants to clean the repo first and pilot a path that is easier to
control. Digital products reduce fulfilment and supplier complexity while still
testing research, product creation, approval packs, quality control, publishing
planning, cost tracking, and performance review.

## Original Consequences

- Stage 2 live research should prioritize digital-product opportunities.
- Stage 3 paid generation should support digital-product assets and approval
  packs first.
- POD/Gelato remains a later guarded path, not the immediate next pilot.

## Historical Review Trigger

Review after the first digital-product approval pack has live research evidence
and a launch recommendation.
