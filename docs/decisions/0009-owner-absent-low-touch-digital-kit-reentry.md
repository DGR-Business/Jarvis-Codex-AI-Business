# 0009 - Owner-Absent Low-Touch Digital Kit Re-entry

Date: 2026-07-29
Status: accepted policy and preparation direction; implementation and commercial
readiness remain in progress

## Decision

Pantheon's next commercial preparation direction is a low-touch digital kit for
social media managers, provisionally named:

> Social Media Manager Client Approval & Scope Guard Kit

This is a new commercial hypothesis and evidence plan. It is not a revision,
retry, renamed continuation, or reopening of the terminally stopped Client
Control and Profitability Workbook.

Pantheon will operate toward an owner-absent model:

- Daniel supplies strategy, guidance, and exact approvals for protected actions;
- Pantheon performs routine research, preparation, production, quality,
  distribution planning, monitoring, and evidence capture when those
  capabilities have been implemented and proved;
- the public venture may be operated under a brand and need not depend on
  Daniel's face, personal voice, or routine participation;
- brand-operated does not mean anonymous, deceptive, or identity-evasive;
- Pantheon must represent the business, offer, sender, caller, and evidence
  truthfully; and
- platform identity checks, KYC, OAuth, MFA, legal acceptance, and other actions
  that genuinely require the account owner remain protected and may still
  require Daniel.

The initial commercial assumptions are explicitly unproven:

- **Buyer hypothesis:** freelance or independent social media managers managing
  retained client work.
- **Problem hypothesis:** approvals, scope changes, revision requests, delivery
  acceptance, and supporting evidence are fragmented across messages and tools,
  creating scope creep, delay, and avoidable disputes.
- **Offer hypothesis:** a practical, low-touch collection of templates,
  checklists, scripts, and logs that helps the buyer control approvals and scope
  without making legal or guaranteed-outcome claims.
- **Price hypothesis:** A$29.
- **Primary channel hypothesis:** Etsy.
- **Fallback channel hypothesis:** Gumroad.

Etsy and Gumroad must each earn selection through current, attributable evidence
about buyer access, channel norms, fees, attribution, account requirements,
operating burden, and expected net cash contribution. Neither is a generic
Pantheon default, and the fallback must not be treated as validated merely
because the primary channel proves unsuitable.

## Active Goal

> Make Pantheon’s current release the canonical, compatibility-verified baseline;
> align what the system reports with what it can actually prove; establish one
> channel-consistent path from a selected offer to attributable buyer and cash
> evidence; prove the owner journey end to end; and prepare the next commercial
> test without contacting customers, publishing, or spending money until
> separately approved.

## Evidence And Proof Contract

Pantheon must support two clearly distinguished routes for real commercial
evidence:

1. **Imported platform evidence:** a marketplace or payment-platform export with
   source, reporting period, import time, file hash, currency, transaction
   identifiers, fees, refunds, and attribution retained.
2. **Manual receipt-backed evidence:** an operator-attested record linked to a
   receipt, invoice, settlement reference, or other retained source, with who
   entered it, when, the evidence location or reference, currency, and
   reconciliation state retained.

Manual evidence is not lower-integrity by definition, but it must never be
presented as a platform import. Estimated costs, cash paid, provider usage,
orders, refunds, revenue, and net cash contribution remain separate facts.

The commercial proof threshold remains:

- three independent paying buyers; and
- positive **actual net cash contribution in AUD** after revenue, refunds,
  marketplace and payment fees, advertising, fulfilment, paid tools, model
  usage, and all other attributable cash costs.

A prepared product, passed test, listing draft, favourite, enquiry, search
volume, model opinion, or estimated margin cannot satisfy this threshold.

## Current Authority Boundary

The current session is preparation-only with A$0 external spend. It may perform
internal, reversible work needed to verify the release, reconcile system truth,
design the evidence contract, conduct read-only diligence, prepare the offer and
test specification, and prove the protected owner journey using dry runs.

It may not:

- contact a prospective or existing customer;
- place or receive a real business call;
- send a buyer email or message;
- create or alter a marketplace, advertising, payment, or business account;
- perform KYC, OAuth, or MFA;
- publish a listing, post, page, advertisement, or public offer;
- activate paid advertising or incur external spend;
- accept a legal agreement or consequential platform term;
- move money; or
- imply that any of those actions occurred.

Voice, email, marketplace, publishing, advertising, customer-service, and
related adapters remain a destination, not present authority. Each must have a
lawful channel, venture-scoped credentials, a dry-run path, a health/status
surface, exact approval rules, idempotency, cost capture, receipts, monitoring,
timeout and unknown-outcome handling, and release proof before live use.

The aspiration that an internet buyer can be reached through an appropriate
channel is not itself evidence. Buyer access must be proved channel by channel
without bypassing identity, consent, platform, privacy, anti-spam, rate-limit,
or other lawful controls.

## Options Considered

### Reopen the stopped workbook

Rejected. Its one permitted evidence recheck returned a terminal non-pass. The
recorded rule forbids another retry, revision, buyer pack, or external-test
decision for that build.

### Require Daniel to sell or deliver the test manually

Rejected as the operating destination. It would make the owner the routine
sales, service, publishing, or fulfilment bottleneck and would not prove the
owner-absent system Daniel requested.

### Begin broad discovery again

Rejected for this session. The current priority is release truth,
compatibility, attributable evidence, and one bounded new hypothesis, not
another unsupported general scan.

### Prepare a separate low-touch kit and evidence path

Accepted. It preserves the terminal result, tests a narrower operational
problem, fits a brand-operated delivery model, and can be evaluated through a
small, attributable commercial test after separate approval.

## Consequences

- The stopped workbook and all its conclusions remain immutable historical
  evidence.
- The new kit receives a new specification, evidence plan, quality record, and
  later test decision if readiness gates pass.
- Pantheon's generic kernel must remain buyer-, product-, format-, and
  channel-neutral.
- Reach failure, offer failure, format failure, price failure, channel failure,
  fulfilment failure, and underlying demand failure must remain distinguishable.
- A channel-specific view threshold or test duration will not be invented in
  advance; current channel evidence must justify them before any external test
  is proposed.
- Passing preparation and release checks will establish system readiness only.
  They will not establish buyer demand, revenue, or business success.
- One venture remains the maximum until the three-buyer and positive actual AUD
  net cash contribution gate is met.
- Narrow, compatible, reversible repairs may proceed inside the approved goal
  with proportional tests and a factual report. A major runtime, framework,
  dependency, schema, or operating-model upgrade requires an explicit scope,
  compatibility evidence, recovery and rollback proof, and visible owner
  review before it can replace the canonical baseline.
- Recovery and migration work must preserve source evidence and create receipts
  for the input, action, output, hashes, integrity checks, and rollback state.
