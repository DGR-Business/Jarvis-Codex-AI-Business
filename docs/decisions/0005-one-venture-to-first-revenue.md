# 0005 - One Venture To First Revenue

Date: 2026-07-14
Status: accepted

## Decision

Jarvis-Codex will prove one digital-product venture through Gumroad Direct before
adding another venture, broader channel automation, or paid media capability.

The proof threshold is three independent paying buyers and positive cash
contribution. The public venture remains faceless and voiceless; Daniel may
complete required platform KYC privately.

Autonomy is promoted per exact capability only after five consecutive reviewed
successes. Publishing, customer contact, spend, account action, legal matters,
disputes, and money movement remain explicit operator decisions or hard stops.

## Consequences

- One venture and one real-world test are active at a time.
- The Demand Validator Agents SDK proof precedes broader worker execution.
- Gumroad account creation and first publication are performed by Daniel after
  reviewing the Publish Pack.
- Initial distribution is at most three compliant organic posts across two
  evidence-selected channels.
- Live search, paid media, POD, additional ventures, and wider autonomy are
  deferred until first proof justifies them.
- Normal operator involvement targets eight hours weekly. Any approved
  intensive week may use up to 16 hours.

## Review Trigger

Review after the first test reaches 14 days or 50 qualified product views, or
earlier if it reaches three independent paid buyers and positive cash
contribution.
