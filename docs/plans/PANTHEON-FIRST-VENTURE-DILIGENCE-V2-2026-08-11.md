## Evidence Standard

The round must apply the Commercial Constitution's ten required investment
criteria: buyer/problem, direct demand, competition/entry, offer/value,
economics, distribution, operations, experiment, alternatives, and risk.

Evidence must meet these minimum rules:

- Retain 10 to 15 relevant offers on one consistently sampled ledger: at least
  four direct or near-direct, three adjacent, two indirect, and two examples for
  each approved format where lawful public evidence exists. Accept no more than
  two counted offers from one seller.

  must retain provenance and may not exceed 30 sampled items in total.
- Prefer Tier 1 official platform, fee, policy, payout, and public-reference
  sources for channel facts. Use Tier 2 established professional evidence and
  Tier 3 methodologically disclosed research or marketplace observations for
  context. Tier 4 discussion and model inference may identify questions but
  cannot carry a material decision alone.
- Keep `observed_fact`, `estimate`, `assumption`, `model_inference`,
  `owner_attestation`, `owner_preference`, `proven_pantheon_learning`, and
  `unknown` visibly distinct.
- Listings, visibility, ratings, reviews, carts, search interest, audience size,
  and model opinion do not prove sales, conversion, profitability, realised
  price, or willingness to pay. Direct-demand support requires attributable
  purchase, transaction, enquiry, verified review, waitlist, or comparable
  behavioural evidence with explicit limitations.
- Reconcile A$19, A$29, and A$39 through revenue, discounts, refunds, platform
  and payment fees, taxes where known, currency conversion, payout/settlement,
  acquisition, production, fulfilment, support, paid tools, model usage, and

  be combined.
- Seek and retain contrary evidence. Every material conclusion must state what
  could reverse it, and unresolved contradictions must block advancement.
- Compare subscription software, free tools, do-it-yourself workflows, the
  three proposed formats, another lawful channel, another Portfolio
  opportunity, and doing nothing. Competitor existence or list price alone is
  not a reason to enter.

## Final Outcome Rules

If evidence and cost custody are complete, the round must record exactly one
versioned result:

- `build`: every required criterion is sufficiently supported for the limited
  next decision, no unresolved contradiction can materially reverse the case,
  and a credible smallest buyer test can be specified. This means only "prepare
  a separately governed minimum-build and buyer-test proposal." It authorises
  no production or external action and does not create, register, activate, or
  imply a Venture Kit or operating venture.
- `research_more`: a decision-critical gap remains, but there is one specific,
  economical, lawful way to resolve it. Record that evidence, maximum cost, and
  decision effect. Do not extend this authority or begin another round.
- `revise`: the buyer/problem direction retains evidence, but the offer, format,
  price, channel, positioning, or operating design is materially contradicted.
  Record the smallest coherent revision and what would test it; do not build it.
- `reject`: demand, entry position, economics, operations, or risk is
  structurally unattractive, or no economical evidence path could make the
  proposed case investable.
- `no_investment`: no candidate format/channel case beats alternatives and
  retaining cash on risk-adjusted expected net cash contribution. This is a
  valid successful result.

Absence of exact-offer willingness-to-pay evidence must remain explicit even if
the result is `build`. Read-only diligence cannot be described as market
validation.
