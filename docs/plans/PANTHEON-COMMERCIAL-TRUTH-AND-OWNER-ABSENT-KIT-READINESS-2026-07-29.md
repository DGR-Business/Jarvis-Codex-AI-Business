## Operating Policy

### Owner-absent, brand-operated

Owner-absent means Daniel provides strategy, guidance, and exact approvals for
protected actions while Pantheon performs routine work after the relevant
capability has been implemented and proved.

The public venture may be brand-operated and need not use Daniel's face,
personal voice, or routine labour. It must not be anonymous in a deceptive or
identity-evasive sense. Pantheon must use truthful sender, caller, brand, offer,
evidence, and business representations. Platform KYC, account ownership, MFA,
OAuth, legal acceptance, and other controls may still require Daniel personally
when the platform or law requires it.

The destination includes lawful, tested adapters for voice calls, email and
messages, marketplaces, publishing, advertising, customer service, fulfilment,
and measurement. A destination capability is not present authority. Before live
use, each adapter must have:

- a venture-scoped purpose and credential reference;
- a dry-run path and operator-visible health/status;
- exact approval and protected-action classification;
- cost and rate limits;
- idempotency, receipts, event logging, and attribution;
- timeout, retry, and unknown-outcome handling;
- privacy, consent, retention, platform, and legal boundaries; and
- automated and real operator-path proof.

“Anyone on the internet can be reached through the correct channels” remains an
aspiration. Each buyer-access route must be evidenced and lawful; Pantheon may
not bypass authentication, CAPTCHA, consent, anti-spam, platform, rate-limit,
privacy, or other technical and legal controls.

### Current A$0 external boundary

This implementation session is preparation-only and has an A$0 external-spend
limit. Internal work may inspect, migrate, test, render, draft, research public
information read-only, and exercise safe dry runs. It may not:

- contact or call a buyer;
- send a buyer email or message;
- create, connect, or alter an external account;
- perform KYC, OAuth, or MFA;
- publish a listing, post, page, advertisement, or offer;
- activate advertising or paid distribution;
- accept legal or consequential platform terms;
- move money; or
- incur external spend.

Every later external action requires its own current evidence, exact scope,
cost, expected effect, approval, and receipt.

### Safe change and recovery discipline

A narrow patch may proceed inside this goal when it is compatible, reversible,
bounded to the observed defect, protected by focused and regression tests, and
does not change business authority or policy. The result must still be reported
and pass the applicable release gates.

A major runtime, framework, dependency, database, security, architecture, or
operating-model upgrade must not be hidden inside a repair. It requires an
explicit scope, current compatibility evidence, migration and rollback design,
recovery proof, test and owner-impact assessment, and visible review before it
can replace the canonical baseline.

Recovery and migration actions must leave a receipt identifying the source
state, exact action, destination, hashes, database integrity and foreign-key
results, artifacts, configuration requirements, and rollback state. Destructive
cleanup remains a separate previewed action.

## Commercial Readiness Gates

The new hypothesis cannot enter product build or protected external-test
preparation until its evidence pack states:

1. the exact buyer and buying context;
2. the painful problem and direct evidence;
3. substitutes, competitors, density, prices, and adverse evidence;
4. the narrow offer and why its format fits the buyer;
5. the evidence gap the smallest test will close;
6. the evidence-selected channel and lawful buyer-access path;
7. price, fees, refunds, fulfilment, support, model/tool, advertising, tax, and
   other attributable cost assumptions;
8. expected net cash contribution in AUD and break-even logic;
9. production and quality requirements;
10. the test cohort, attribution source, reach measure, result measure, deadline,
    and diagnose/revise/stop rules;
11. operational, platform, privacy, legal, and brand risks; and
12. why this test is better than retaining cash or testing another opportunity.

Search demand, model opinion, population size, adjacent sales, favourites,
enquiries, or an estimated margin may support a decision but cannot prove
willingness to pay or actual contribution.

## Buyer And Cash Evidence Contract

Pantheon must support two explicit evidence routes without merging their
provenance.

### Route A - Imported platform evidence

An imported marketplace, payment, or settlement report must retain:

- platform and account reference;
- exact source file, API response, or export type;
- reporting period and import time;
- content hash and immutable import receipt;
- order or transaction identity sufficient for deduplication;
- buyer-independence basis without exposing unnecessary personal data;
- gross revenue, refunds, platform fees, payment fees, taxes, settlement, and
  currency;
- AUD conversion source and rate where needed;
- offer, channel, campaign, and experiment attribution; and
- reconciliation and correction history.

### Route B - Manual receipt-backed evidence

A manual record must retain:

- operator attestation and entry time;
- source type and retained receipt, invoice, settlement reference, or evidence
  location;
- transaction identity sufficient for deduplication;
- buyer-independence basis without unnecessary personal data;
- gross revenue, refunds, fees, taxes, settlement, and currency;
- AUD conversion source and rate where needed;
- offer, channel, campaign, and experiment attribution;
- reconciliation state and reason for manual entry; and
- append-only revision or reversal history.

The dashboard must label the route plainly. A manual record must not look like
an imported platform transaction. An estimate must not look settled.

### Proof calculation

The investment proof requires:

- at least three independent paying buyers; and
- positive actual net cash contribution in AUD:

`cash revenue - refunds - platform fees - payment fees - advertising - fulfilment - paid tools - model usage - other attributable cash costs`

The calculation must state the cohort, period, inclusions, exclusions, currency
conversion, and evidence status. Positive fee-only margin is insufficient.

  money movement, or external spend occurred; and
- the factual release record and next protected decision are durable.

Passing these criteria establishes preparation and system readiness. It does
not establish buyer demand, revenue, positive contribution, or commercial
success.
