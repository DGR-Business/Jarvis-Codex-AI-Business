# Pantheon Full-Journey Proof And First Product

Date: 2026-07-22
Status: completed on 2026-07-24
Owner: Daniel
Technical steward: Jarvis (Codex)

## Goal

Make Pantheon complete and prove one uninterrupted commercial journey from
broad market discovery to a genuine, publication-ready digital-product range.
Run an isolated rehearsal first, then a clean production-intent journey. Use
Luna for every specialist with no silent Terra or Sol fallback, keep combined
AI and tool exposure below the approved ceiling, and stop before any account, publication,
customer-contact, advertising, legal, or money action.

Daniel amended the combined ceiling from A$15 to A$30 on 2026-07-24 after the
first honest stop showed the original ceiling could not fund both required
runs. The A$100 monthly mandate and all per-action protections remained
unchanged.

Completion requires a credible three-to-six-item catalogue or bundle and a
complete Gumroad store kit, not an arbitrary single item.

## Architecture

- Pantheon owns durable commercial state, evidence, money, approvals, schedules,
  artifacts, recovery, and dashboard truth.
- The OpenAI Agents SDK performs bounded specialist reasoning. Each handoff is a
  separately persisted stage rather than one fragile long conversation.
- The pre-publication team is Opportunity Scout, Demand Validator, Finance
  Agent, Offer Architect, Product Builder, Quality Reviewer, Copy and Conversion
  Agent, Distribution Agent, and Chief of Staff.
- Customer Voice and Growth remain inactive until real market or customer data
  exists.
- The fixed roster remains. Dynamic temporary teams are later work.
- The first venture-kit implementation is Digital Product Kit. Later kits may
  support POD, affiliate, white-label, SaaS, and other models without changing
  the kernel.
- Verified stage completion queues the next bounded stage. The scheduler remains
  recovery infrastructure.

## Journey Contract

- Persist mode, active stage, locked model, combined cap, candidate
  shortlist, selected opportunity, outputs, corrections, and terminal status.
- Keep rehearsal database and artifacts separate from production.
- Record the Luna override in every task, approval, trace, cost receipt, and
  result.
- Count reservations, incurred estimates, and unknown outcomes against the
  combined cap.
- Discover broadly, retain all lawful findings, and advance three buildable
  digital-product candidates to comparable validation.
- Select the strongest candidate from recorded evidence, commercial score,
  risk, economics, and execution fit; explain the winner and alternatives.
- Reject commercially empty answers even when their JSON shape is valid.
- Allow one targeted correction per stage. Unknown provider outcomes stop with
  no automatic paid retry.

## Digital Product Kit

The Product Builder must create:

- genuine customer files and a manifest;
- an evidence-justified three-to-six-product catalogue or bundle;
- truthful cover art and storefront previews derived from the actual files;
- Gumroad title, description, price, tags, FAQ, and included-files summary;
- no more than three proposed launch posts across two selected channels;
- tracking fields, stop rules, and a short Gumroad upload checklist.

Quality Reviewer must open the exact files, verify hashes and formats, inspect
rendered previews, check claims, and reject placeholder, broken, or misleading
material. Chief of Staff creates one concise operator brief from verified
records and cannot change runtime truth or authorize publication.
