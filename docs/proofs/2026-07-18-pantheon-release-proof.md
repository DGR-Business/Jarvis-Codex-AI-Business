## Commercial Result

The Scout ranked:

1. a niche small-business bookkeeping and cash-flow template;
2. a wedding-planning spreadsheet bundle;
3. an ADHD-friendly executive-function system.

The evidence showed visible marketplace supply, review activity, buyer language,
and price signals. The Validators found credible category demand but also strong
competition and price pressure. The evidence did not establish Pantheon's own
conversion rate, contribution margin, independent buyers, or willingness to pay.

Pantheon therefore stopped at `Needs direction`. It did not treat source count,
reviews, or model confidence as revenue proof and did not start production
automatically. That is the correct commercial outcome.

## Honest Limits

This release does not prove that Pantheon has made money or can yet run unattended
across every online channel.

- No real product has been selected, published, or sold.
- Gumroad is intentionally not connected before opportunity selection and
  Daniel's private account/KYC action.
- Product Builder's local file and Code Interpreter paths are tested, but its
  first paid production assignment and independent live quality review remain a
  separate commercial run.
- Image generation is configured but has not received a paid production proof.
- Social posting, advertising activation, customer contact, account setup,
  refunds, contracts, and money movement remain protected.
- Marketplace research can support a hypothesis; it cannot replace platform
  sales data, qualified views, conversion, refunds, and cash contribution.
- Dynamic manager-created teams, cloud operation, and mobile control remain
  later capabilities, not hidden claims.

Pantheon is ready to start the real loop. It is not yet a proven autonomous
business.
