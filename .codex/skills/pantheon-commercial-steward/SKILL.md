# Pantheon Commercial Steward

## Status and authority

This is an existing, intentional Codex-only commercial-context skill. It
supplements the seven shared Pantheon skills and cannot override or weaken
`AGENTS.md`, an owner-approved Phase Pack, its approved Work Package, relevant
ADRs or non-specialized Master constraints.

Use it only when the current approved package contains substantive commercial-
system work. A package may not silently absorb an unrelated repair, commercial
policy change or external action.

## Ground truth

Read only what the authorized task needs:

- the approved Work Package, its owner-approved Phase Pack, relevant ADRs and
  `docs/v2/PANTHEON_V2_MASTER_PLAN.md` for programme scope;
- `config/commercial-constitution.js` for mandatory investment criteria,
  decision rules, source tiers and model roles;
- `config/commercial-knowledge-v1.js` for cited propositions relevant to the
  work; and
- current package evidence and v2 progress/blocker/handoff records for proven
  results, failures and unresolved work.

Legacy `docs/Pantheon Master Plan.md` and `docs/Pantheon Build Log.md` may retain
historical evidence, but they are not active programme authority or the current
continuity ledger. Do not treat chat history, model opinion, doctrine, search
interest or aggregate population as venture-specific proof.

## Method

1. State the commercial outcome affected: demand, offer, distribution,
   conversion, fulfilment, feedback, unit economics or support infrastructure.
2. Identify the exact buyer, problem, offer, channel, price or cost assumption,
   evidence standard, next money move and kill or revision rule when the work is
   commercial.
3. Separate observed facts, estimates, assumptions, model inference and proven
   Pantheon learning.
4. Compare the proposed change with alternatives and doing nothing.
5. Prefer the smallest architecture that preserves truth, recovery,
   measurement and operator simplicity.
6. Keep generic kernel behavior venture-neutral. Put business-model-specific
   production and operating rules in a registered Venture Kit.
7. Reject hardcoded platform or product assumptions in generic APIs.
8. Record actual and estimated costs separately in AUD and use net cash
   contribution as the primary outcome.
9. Run only the verification specified by the Package and Pack. A full suite or
   interactive browser proof is required only when that contract requires it.
10. Record evidence in the package completion/progress records. When evidence
    changes the roadmap, propose an ADR and owner-reviewed plan amendment; do
    not silently edit the stable Master or revive the legacy Build Log as
    current authority.

## Protected actions

Business-policy changes, external actions, publishing, account/KYC/OAuth/MFA
work, legal agreements, customer contact, money movement and undelegated spend
remain protected. Provider discovery does not authorize installation,
credentials, terms, live authority or spend.

Never force an opportunity through production. `no_investment` is a valid
successful result when supported by the required evidence.
