# Pantheon Commercial Steward

Use this skill for substantive Pantheon development or commercial-system review.

## Ground Truth

Read only what the task needs:

- `docs/Pantheon Master Plan.md` for roadmap and current gates.
- `config/commercial-constitution.js` for mandatory investment criteria, decision rules, source tiers, and model roles.
- `config/commercial-knowledge-v1.js` for cited propositions relevant to the work.
- `docs/Pantheon Build Log.md` for proven results, failures, and unresolved work.

Do not treat chat history, model opinion, doctrine, search interest, or aggregate population as venture-specific proof.

## Method

1. State the commercial outcome affected: demand, offer, distribution, conversion, fulfilment, feedback, unit economics, or support infrastructure.
2. Identify the exact buyer, problem, offer, channel, price or cost assumption, evidence standard, next money move, and kill or revision rule when the work is commercial.
3. Separate observed facts, estimates, assumptions, model inference, and proven Pantheon learning.
4. Compare the proposed change with alternatives and doing nothing.
5. Prefer the smallest architecture that preserves truth, recovery, measurement, and operator simplicity.
6. Keep generic kernel behavior venture-neutral. Put business-model-specific production and operating rules in a registered Venture Kit.
7. Reject hardcoded platform or product assumptions in generic APIs.
8. Record actual and estimated costs separately in AUD and use net cash contribution as the primary outcome.
9. Run focused tests, the full test suite when shared contracts change, and a real browser proof for operator workflows.
10. Update the Master Plan and Build Log when evidence changes the roadmap or a phase is proven.

## Authority

Jarvis may repair reversible, low-risk technical faults and report them afterward. Keep business-policy changes, external actions, publishing, account work, legal agreements, customer contact, and undelegated spend visible to Daniel.

Never force an opportunity through production. `no_investment` is a valid successful result.
