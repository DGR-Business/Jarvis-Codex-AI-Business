## Entity Structure — Questions for Your Accountant/Solicitor

> **DISCLAIMER:** The questions below are based on research only, not legal or tax advice. Discuss these with a registered tax agent, accountant, or solicitor before making any decisions.

The following questions are informed by the research in `holding-company/entity-research.md` and `holding-company/finance/tax-obligations.md`. They are designed to help structure an initial consultation with a professional adviser.

1. **What entity type best suits a solo online business portfolio?**
   - Currently pre-revenue, running AI-automated POD (print-on-demand) via Etsy/Redbubble
   - Low startup costs (mainly software subscriptions)
   - No employees, no physical inventory held

2. **At what revenue point should I move from sole trader to company or trust?**
   - GST registration threshold is currently $75,000 turnover (may increase — check latest ATO guidance)
   - Company tax rate (25% for base rate entities) vs personal marginal rates
   - When does the cost of compliance justify the tax/liability benefits?

3. **Can one entity run all ventures, or should each be separate?**
   - Sole trader can register multiple business names under one ABN
   - Company can operate multiple business lines
   - Risk isolation: does mixing ventures create cross-liability exposure?

4. **What is the most tax-efficient structure for my situation?**
   - Sole trader simplicity vs trust distribution flexibility vs company rate advantages
   - Impact of franking credits if using a company
   - Superannuation considerations for a sole operator

5. **Do I need asset protection?**
   - Selling physical products (POD) — what liability exposure exists?
   - Platform Terms of Service (Etsy, Redbubble) and their indemnification clauses
   - Intellectual property considerations for design-based products

6. **What registrations do I need right now (pre-revenue)?**
   - ABN — likely needed for platform seller registration
   - GST — not mandatory below $75,000 but voluntary registration has pros/cons
   - Business name registration if trading under a name other than personal name

## Venture Selection Criteria
A venture is worth pursuing if it meets all of these:
- Fully digital or drop-shipped (no inventory held by operator)
- Can be launched with under $100 AUD in setup costs
- AI can handle 80%+ of recurring work (design, copy, research, listing)
- Operator role is review and approval only, not execution
- Has a clear exit — can be paused or shut down with no ongoing obligations

## Success Metrics
- Ventures are measured on: revenue, profit margin, operator hours per week, AI automation rate
- A venture is healthy if operator time is under 2 hours/week at steady state
- A venture is ready to scale if it is profitable and repeatable
- A venture is archived if it has not generated revenue within 6 months of going live

## Venture Priority Order
When resources (operator time, budget) are constrained:
1. Active live ventures come first — protect existing revenue
2. Ventures closest to launch come second
3. New ideas are only started once the current venture reaches Stage 2 (Live)
