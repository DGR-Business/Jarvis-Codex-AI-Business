## Niche Research Priorities
1. Evergreen niches with consistent year-round demand
2. Low-competition niches (< 50,000 Etsy results)
3. Niches where AI-generated designs are competitive
4. Niches with emotional/identity-driven purchases (hobbies, professions, pet owners)

## Research Schedule
- **Weekly:** Quick trend scan (Phase 1 only) during Strategic Pulse
- **Bi-weekly:** Full 5-phase research cycle on top candidates
- **On-demand:** When operator drops niche ideas in IDEAS.md
