## Phase 4 — Competitive Mapping

### Niche 1: Dog/Cat Mom Illustrated Sweatshirt — Top Sellers & Gaps

**Top seller patterns observed on Etsy (via search result listings, April 2026):**
- Most top listings use text-only designs ("Just a Girl Who Loves Her Dogs", "Dog Mom", "Best Dog Mom Ever")
- Personalised variants (custom dog name, breed name on sleeve) are growing but use generic fonts
- "Treat Dealer" embroidered sweatshirt (Etsy listing 1885049506) is performing well — shows humour-plus-identity works
- "Dog MILF" sweatshirt (listing 1576337455) has strong social-media-shareable appeal

**Competitive gaps:**
1. Illustrated breed-specific design (e.g. golden retriever with flower crown, watercolor style) — few listings combine illustration + "dog mom" phrasing at quality
2. "Cat mum + Dog mum" dual-identity sweatshirt with illustrated cats and dogs — underserved
3. Mother's Day specific framing: "Happy Mother's Day to every dog mom" with illustration — seasonal + identity
4. Australian angle: "Dog Mum" (AU spelling) — small but real differentiation for AU buyers; seller can also run "Dog Mom" for US

**Design style that wins:** Warm watercolor illustration of the pet (realistic breed rendering in soft watercolour) with bold clean serif/sans-serif typography. NOT cartoon. Think "artisan gift shop" quality, not meme-style.

### Niche 2: Birth Flower / Wildflower Tote Bag — Top Sellers & Gaps

**Top seller patterns observed:**
- Etsy listing 1810719351: "Wildflower Tote Bag, Floral Botanical Tote, Mothers Day Gift" — active listing, demonstrates buyer intent exists
- /market/wildflower_tote_bag and /market/tote_bag_floral both active with multiple listings
- Most top floral totes: generic wildflower bunches, text not integrated
- Birth flower mugs are more crowded than birth flower totes — opportunity to lead on the tote format

**Competitive gaps:**
1. Birth month flower tote with name personalisation AND "Happy Mother's Day" framing — combination underserved
2. "She is wildflower" or similar botanical affirmation with specific May birth flower (lily of the valley) — seasonal + personal
3. Watercolor illustration that covers the full tote face (not just a small centred design) — premium visual impact, commands higher price

**Design style that wins:** Full-face watercolor botanical illustration. Specific birth month flower rendered in loose, expressive watercolor with elegant script typography. Warm, earthy palette with one accent (sage green + blush + cream).

### Niche 3: BookTok / Dark Academia Mug — Top Sellers & Gaps

**Top seller patterns observed:**
- Listings 1427717779, 1413524618: "Slow Burn Club", "Bookish Gifts Dark Academia Literature Fantasy" — active, high-review listings
- Most use text-based designs on dark background (dark academia aesthetic via font/colour only)

1. Illustrated cosy reading scene on mug (stack of books, candle, window with rain, cat, tea — full illustrated scene wrapping the mug) — almost no one is doing illustration-first bookish mugs at quality

3. Dark academia mug with a witty literary quote in ornate serif typography + subtle illustrated border (quill, parchment, open book) — aesthetic upgrade on the text-only competition

**Design style that wins:** Cosy illustrated vignette OR dark academia ornate typography with illustration elements (not text-only). Style choice: Cozy Fantasy (warm ochres, deep greens, candlelight) OR Dark Academia (deep navy/burgundy, serif fonts, gothic border elements). GPT Image 2 excels at both.

## Risks & Caveats

| Risk | Level | Detail |
|------|-------|--------|
| EverBee data not captured | MEDIUM | Chrome extension was offline. Revenue estimates are based on editorial sources and case studies, not live listing analytics. Actual top-listing revenues could be higher or lower. Recommend re-running EverBee validation when Chrome extension is back online. |
| Mother's Day shipping window | HIGH | Mother's Day AU is May 11. Mother's Day US is May 11, 2025 — wait, confirmed 2026 US Mother's Day is May 10. With ~16 days to listing, standard POD shipping (5–7 business days US domestic) means listings published within 2–3 days still catch late shoppers. Etsy buyers increasingly order late. Priority: publish by April 28. |
| Dog/cat breed illustrations — IP risk | LOW | Generic breed illustrations (golden retriever, tabby cat) are not IP. Avoid: specific named characters, branded dog collars in illustration, Disney-adjacent anything. |
| "Dog Mom" trademark | LOW-MEDIUM | "Dog Mom" as a phrase is not trademarkable in common use. "I Am a Dog Mom" specific phrasing — conduct USPTO quick search before publishing. Recommend checking via USPTO TESS. Flag: Human-Only determination if any trademark is found. |
| Offsite ads fee reducing margins | MEDIUM | Etsy's offsite ads are mandatory for shops that have made $10K+ in sales. For a new shop, they are opt-in. Recommend keeping offsite ads OFF until review volume justifies the traffic trade-off. |
| POD supplier quality variance | MEDIUM | Printify has multiple suppliers per product — quality varies. Always order a sample before publishing. Especially for sweatshirts where softness and print placement matter for the illustrated design. |
| Australian GST | MEDIUM | Once AU revenue exceeds AUD $75,000 threshold, GST registration is required. Below that threshold, Etsy collects and remits GST on AU sales under marketplace facilitator rules. Flag: consult accountant before threshold is reached. NOT legal/financial advice — professional review required. |
| Seasonal decay | LOW for sweatshirts, MEDIUM for tote | Sweatshirts and mugs are year-round gifting products. Birth flower tote has strong Mother's Day peak but remains relevant for birthdays all year. |
