## Product 1 — Night Shift Nurse Crewneck Sweatshirt

### 1. Final Chosen Phrase
**"Night Shift Nurse: Fuelled by Coffee, Spite, and the Sound of Monitors"**

Why: More specific than the alternative — "coffee, spite, monitors" hits three distinct nurse identity points (caffeine dependency, dark resilience, clinical setting). Reads as a vertical stack naturally. Stronger emotional hook.

---

### 2. Image Generation Prompt

**For ChatGPT (DALL-E) or Midjourney:**

```
Flat lay apparel graphic design for a dark heather grey crewneck sweatshirt. Clean, modern typographic print design — no cartoon characters, no clipart, no drop shadows, no gradients. White and pale blue text only.

Layout: vertically centred chest placement, stacked text block.
- Line 1 (small caps, tracking wide): NIGHT SHIFT NURSE
- Line 2 (large bold display font): "Fuelled by Coffee,"
- Line 3 (large bold display font): "Spite,"
- Line 4 (large bold display font): "and the Sound"
- Line 5 (large bold display font): "of Monitors"
- Small minimal icon: a single crescent moon outline OR a minimalist ECG/heart monitor flatline with one pulse peak, placed either above line 1 or centred below line 5. The icon must be outline-only, no fill.

Style: editorial streetwear typography, like a band merch graphic or ACLU tote. Clean. No border frame. No decorative flourishes. Transparent background for the print element.

Do NOT include: stethoscopes, clipart nurses, syringes, pill icons, cartoons, drop shadows, bevels, glows.
```

---

### 3. Canva / Design Spec

**Fonts:**
- Line 1 header (small caps label): Montserrat ExtraBold or Inter Bold, all caps, letter-spacing +200
- Main text lines 2-5: Big Shoulders Display (free on Google Fonts) ExtraBold, or Oswald Bold as fallback
- Alternative pairing if those unavailable: Barlow Condensed Black + Barlow Regular

**Colours:**
- Background: transparent (print on garment)
- Primary text: #FFFFFF (white)
- Accent text or icon: #A8C4D4 (pale ice blue) — use sparingly on one line or the icon only

**Layout:**
- Canvas size: 4500 x 5400 px
- Text block centred horizontally
- Top of text block starts at approximately y=1500px (upper chest placement)
- Line 1 (NIGHT SHIFT NURSE): ~120px font size at 4500px canvas width
- Lines 2-5 (main phrase): ~340-380px font size — adjust so the block spans roughly 2800px wide
- Icon (if used): 300x300px, placed 80px above Line 1 or 80px below Line 5
- No border, no background fill, no decorative elements

**Colour swatch for garment:**
- Dark heather grey: Gildan 18000 in Dark Heather (#3D3D3D approx)
- Navy: Gildan 18000 Navy (#1B2A4A approx)
- Black: Gildan 18000 Black (#111111)

## Product 2 — Sarcastic Nurse Coffee Mug (11oz)

### 1. Final Chosen Phrase
**"Coffee: Because Stabbing People is Frowned Upon (But I Have a Needle License)"**

Why: The parenthetical punchline is the payoff — it's unexpected and specific to nurses (the needle licence callback). More memorable than the "doctors being wrong" variant, which reads more generic. Will perform well as a gift item; the buyer immediately imagines giving it to a nurse friend.

---

### 2. Image Generation Prompt

**For ChatGPT (DALL-E) or Midjourney:**

```
Flat graphic design for printing on a white 11oz ceramic mug. Black text and line art on a white background. No gradients, no colour fills, no 3D effects.

Layout: single front panel, centred vertically and horizontally.
- Main text, large and bold: "COFFEE:"
- Subtext below, medium weight: "Because Stabbing People"
- Subtext continuation: "is Frowned Upon"
- Parenthetical punchline in smaller italics: "(But I Have a Needle License)"
- Small accent illustration: a minimalist line-art coffee cup (outline only, no fill) with a single wisp of steam, OR a syringe outline — placed above the main text. Keep it small, not dominant.

Style: clean editorial humour print — think New Yorker cartoon meets modern typographic poster. Slightly playful but not childish. Not clipart.

Do NOT include: cartoons, gradients, colour fills, stethoscopes, any medical cross symbols, drop shadows.
Output: black on white, transparent background version preferred for adaptability.
```

---

### 3. Canva / Design Spec

**Fonts:**
- "COFFEE:" headline: Playfair Display Black or Abril Fatface (serif with personality)
- Body lines: Montserrat SemiBold or Raleway Medium
- Parenthetical punchline: Montserrat Italic Regular, slightly smaller size

**Colours:**
- Primary text and icon: #1A1A1A (near-black)
- Background: transparent (white mug surface shows through)
- Optional: a very faint warm tint #FFF8F0 as a background wash if going print-on-white

**Layout:**
- Canvas size: 3600 x 1800 px (standard 11oz mug wrap)
- For front panel only: work within a 1600 x 1400 px centred zone
- Icon (coffee cup or syringe): approx 280 x 280 px
- "COFFEE:" headline: ~220px font size
- Body lines: ~120px font size
- Punchline: ~90px font size, italic
- Total text block should sit in the horizontal middle of the wrap

**For black mug variant:** invert to #FFFFFF text on #000000 background. Same layout, swap colours.

## Product 3 — ICU/NICU Nurse T-Shirt (Comfort Colors 1717)

### 1. Final Chosen Phrase
**"NICU Nurse: Tiny Patients. Massive Heart. Zero Sleep."**

Why: The three-beat rhythm (tiny/massive/zero) is punchy and memorable. Each beat contrasts — it builds to the dark punchline ("Zero Sleep") without being mean-spirited. The NICU angle is more emotionally resonant and less saturated than generic ICU. Strong gift purchase motivation — NICU nurses are extremely proud of their unit.

Variant (if operator prefers ICU): **"ICU People at Their Worst. I Still Show Up."** — more defiant tone, slightly darker. Good alternative if launching two SKUs.

---

### 2. Image Generation Prompt

**For ChatGPT (DALL-E) or Midjourney:**

```
Vintage-style typographic apparel graphic for a terracotta crewneck t-shirt. Warm, slightly distressed retro aesthetic — like a 1970s collegiate sports tee or a vintage National Park poster. Warm off-white and cream text on transparent background.

Layout: centred chest print, stacked text block.
- Top arc or straight line (small, wide-tracked): NICU NURSE
- Large central text: "Tiny Patients."
- Second large line: "Massive Heart."
- Third large line: "Zero Sleep."
- Optional small decorative element: a tiny vintage-style heart outline OR a small star cluster, placed between the title line and the main text block. Hairline only, no fill.

Style: distressed vintage collegiate. Slightly textured type (not clean vector). Think Grateful Dead meets nursing school. Warm cream/ivory/off-white colour palette only. No bright white.

Do NOT include: baby clipart, stethoscopes, literal NICU equipment imagery, modern flat design, clean vector look, gradients, colour fills.
Output: transparent background, print-ready.
```

---

### 3. Canva / Design Spec

**Fonts:**
- "NICU NURSE" top label: Bebas Neue, wide letter-spacing +300, small size
- Main lines ("Tiny Patients." etc.): Playfair Display Black Italic OR Canva's "Freight Display" — gives the vintage editorial feel
- Alternative if those unavailable: Abril Fatface for the main lines, Montserrat Bold for the label

**Distress effect (optional):**
- Apply a subtle grain/noise texture overlay at 15-20% opacity over the text in Canva (use a free Canva texture element, search "grain overlay")
- This mimics the vintage screen-print look without specialist software

**Colours:**
- Primary text: #F5ECD7 (warm cream/ivory)
- Accent / decorative element: #E8D5B0 (slightly darker cream for contrast)
- Garment base (for reference): Comfort Colors 1717 in Terracotta (#C26A4C approx), or Sage (#7D9E8C approx), or Ivory (#F3EBD3 approx)

**Layout:**
- Canvas size: 4500 x 5400 px
- Text block centred horizontally
- Top label "NICU NURSE": ~110px font size, y=1600px
- Decorative element: ~200 x 120px, y=1750px
- Main lines: ~360-400px font size each, line spacing tight (~1.0)
- Total block should span approx 3000px wide, centred at x=2250px

## Notes for Operator

**Which AI tool to use:**
- ChatGPT (DALL-E): All three prompts above — better for typographic/text-dominant designs
- Midjourney: Better if you want more texture and vintage feel for Product 3. Use the same prompt with `--ar 3:4 --style raw --v 6` appended

**After generating:**
- AI-generated images will likely come out at 1024-2048px. You will need to upscale to the target dimensions before uploading to Printify/Printful.
- Free upscaling: upscale.media (fast, good for simple graphics) or bigjpg.com (better for detailed artwork)
- Target: 300 DPI at print dimensions. For the sweatshirt/tshirt designs that means 4500x5400px minimum.

**If you prefer Canva over AI generation:**
- All three designs can be built entirely in Canva using the font and colour specs above
- Use Canva's "Print" workspace, set custom dimensions to match the print file specs
- Download as PNG, transparent background

**Listing priority:** Launch Product 1 (Night Shift Sweatshirt) first — sweatshirts have higher AOV than mugs and t-shirts, and the night shift angle is a strong emotional hook with proven search volume.
