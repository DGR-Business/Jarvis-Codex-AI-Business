## Gelato+ Subscription Break-Even

Gelato+ costs ~$36 AUD/month. Provides up to 20% product discount.

At 20% COGS reduction on sweatshirt ($43 → $34.40 AUD), net profit rises from $20.02 → $28.62 per unit (+$8.60).

Break-even on Gelato+ subscription: **ceil(36 / 8.60) = 5 sweatshirts/month** to justify the subscription.

At Scenario A (5 units) it breaks even. At Scenario B+ it's a clear win. Recommend subscribing once hitting 5+ sweatshirt sales/month.
