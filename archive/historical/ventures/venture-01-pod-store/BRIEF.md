# Venture Brief

## Name
POD Store (Print-on-Demand)

## Description
An automated print-on-demand store operating on Etsy and Redbubble. Designs are created and listed with AI assistance, with fulfilment handled by the platforms' POD partners. The goal is to build a low-overhead, largely automated revenue stream.

## Stage
Idea

## Goals
- Launch an automated Etsy/Redbubble store with AI-generated designs
- Build a repeatable workflow for design creation, listing, and optimisation
- Generate passive income with minimal manual intervention
- Learn the POD business model and iterate based on sales data

## Target Market
Etsy and Redbubble buyers looking for unique, niche-specific designs on apparel, stickers, home decor, and accessories.
