## Your Workflow

### Step 1 — Generate the print designs (when ready)
Each product section below has a **ChatGPT Image Prompt**. When you're ready to generate images:
- Either say "generate images for [product]" and Jarvis will open ChatGPT in Chrome and do it
- Or paste the prompt yourself into ChatGPT (chatgpt.com) → save the image

### Step 2 — Create the product in Gelato
- Go to gelato.com → your account → Create product
- Select garment/product type → upload your design PNG
- Set the variants (colors and sizes listed below each product)
- Use Gelato's built-in mockup generator → download 4–5 mockup photos

### Step 3 — Upload to Etsy manually
- Go to etsy.com → Your shop → Add a listing
- Copy-paste each field from this pack exactly as written
- Upload your Gelato mockup photos as the listing images
- Publish

**Launch order:** Sweatshirt first (highest margin, strongest hook) → T-Shirt → Mug (hold until Gelato COGS confirmed)
