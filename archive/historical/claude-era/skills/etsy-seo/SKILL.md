# Etsy SEO Optimization

Rules and patterns for optimizing Etsy product listings for search visibility and conversion.

## Etsy Search Algorithm Factors

Etsy ranks listings based on these factors (in rough priority order):

1. **Query matching** — how well your title, tags, categories, and attributes match the search query
2. **Listing quality score** — based on click-through rate, favourites, purchases from search
3. **Recency** — newer and recently renewed listings get a temporary boost
4. **Customer experience score** — shop reviews, complete policies, response time
5. **Shipping price** — free shipping and lower shipping prices rank higher
6. **Relevancy** — Etsy's ML model predicting purchase likelihood

## Title Rules

- **Max length:** 140 characters
- **Structure:** Primary keyword first, then secondary keywords, then descriptive modifiers
- **Format:** `[Primary Keyword] [Secondary Keyword] [Product Type] [Modifier] [Occasion/Use]`
- **Use natural language** — not keyword stuffing. Etsy's algorithm understands context.
- **Include 2-3 long-tail keywords** that buyers actually search for
- **Avoid:** all caps, excessive punctuation, trademark terms you don't own

### Title Examples

**Good:**
- `Custom Pet Portrait Digital Download, Watercolor Dog Art Print, Personalized Pet Memorial Gift`
- `Funny Cat T-Shirt, Cute Kitten Graphic Tee, Cat Lover Birthday Gift for Women`
- `Budget Planner Spreadsheet, Monthly Expense Tracker Google Sheets Template, Financial Organizer`

**Bad:**
- `AMAZING PET PORTRAIT!!! BEST QUALITY DIGITAL ART PRINT` (caps, vague, no keywords)
- `Portrait` (too short, no context)
- `Dog Cat Pet Portrait Art Print Wall Art Home Decor Gift` (keyword stuffing, no natural flow)

## Tag Rules

- **Exactly 13 tags** per listing (use all 13 — leaving any empty hurts visibility)
- **Max 20 characters** per tag
- **Multi-word tags** are better than single words (`cat lover gift` not `cat`)
- **Do NOT repeat words from the title** in tags — Etsy already indexes the title
- **Use long-tail phrases** that buyers actually search for
- **Include:**
  - Synonyms for your main keywords
  - Related occasion/use terms (birthday gift, Christmas present, housewarming)
  - Style descriptors (minimalist, boho, vintage, modern)
  - Material/format terms (digital download, printable, instant download)
  - Audience terms (gift for her, gift for mom, cat lover)

### Tag Generation Process

1. Start with 3-4 primary keyword variations (synonyms, alternate phrasings)
2. Add 3-4 occasion/use tags (birthday, Christmas, housewarming, thank you)
3. Add 2-3 audience tags (gift for her, cat mom, pet owner)
4. Add 2-3 style/format tags (digital download, wall art, printable)
5. Fill remaining with long-tail niche phrases

### Tag Example

For a "Custom Pet Portrait Digital Download":
```
1. pet memorial art
2. dog portrait custom
3. cat watercolor print
4. personalized pet gift
5. animal illustration
6. fur baby artwork
7. gift for pet lover
8. loss of pet sympathy
9. digital wall art
10. printable portrait
11. custom dog drawing
12. pet owner birthday
13. animal lover present
```

## Description Best Practices

- **First 40 characters** are critical — they show in search results
- **Structure:** Hook → What it is → What's included → How to use → Specifications
- **Include keywords naturally** in the first 2-3 sentences
- **Bullet points** for specifications and what's included
- **Call to action** at the end
- **Length:** 300-800 words is optimal

### Description Template

```
[Opening hook with primary keyword — 1-2 sentences]

[What the product is and why it's special — 2-3 sentences]

WHAT'S INCLUDED:
• [Item 1 with specs]
• [Item 2 with specs]
• [Bonus items if any]

HOW IT WORKS:
1. [Step 1]
2. [Step 2]
3. [Step 3]

SPECIFICATIONS:
• Format: [PNG/PDF/XLSX etc.]
• Size: [Dimensions]
• Resolution: [DPI]
• [Other relevant specs]

PERFECT FOR:
• [Use case 1]
• [Use case 2]
• [Use case 3]

[Closing with call to action — save to favourites, visit shop for more]
```

## Category and Attributes

- **Always select the most specific category** available
- **Fill in ALL attributes** Etsy offers for your category (colour, size, occasion, etc.)
- Attributes are indexed for search — missing attributes = missing search visibility

## Listing Quality Optimization

- **10 photos** per listing (use all 10 slots)
- **Photo 1** = hero shot (this drives click-through from search results)
- **Video** = Etsy prioritizes listings with video
- **Free shipping** = significant ranking boost
- **Competitive pricing** = Etsy shows price comparisons in search

## Renewal and Recency

- New listings get a ~24-48 hour boost
- Renewed listings get a smaller boost
- Don't renew all listings at once — stagger for continuous visibility
- Seasonal listings: renew 4-6 weeks before the relevant season

## POD-Specific SEO Notes

- For print-on-demand: emphasize the design/art, not the blank product
- Include product type keywords (t-shirt, mug, tote bag) but lead with the design concept
- Cross-list the same design across multiple product types (each is a separate listing with unique SEO)
- Digital downloads: always include "instant download" and "printable" in tags

## Research Process

When researching a niche for SEO:
1. Search the primary keyword on Etsy
2. Note autocomplete suggestions (these are real search queries)
3. Check top 10 results: their titles, tags, prices, reviews
4. Look at "Related searches" at the bottom of results
5. Note the listing count (competition level)
6. Check EverBee data if available (revenue estimates, search volume)
