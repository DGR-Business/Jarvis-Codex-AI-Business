# Pantheon Commercial Proof

- Keep test, owner-created, prospective-customer and independent-customer evidence distinct.
- Verify real transactions and external customer independence.
- Reconcile revenue, fees, refunds, fulfilment and attributable spend.
- Record attribution confidence, data limitations, diagnosis and learning.
- Do not infer demand or success from views, ratings, clicks, generated fixtures or manually inserted events.
- Preserve failed-test learning and recommend scale, revise, pause or kill.
